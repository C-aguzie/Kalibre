About Kalibre

We're ML engineers and data scientists helping Nigerian businesses and startups understand and deploy artificial intelligence — one session at a time.

Our Mission

Most businesses in Nigeria know AI is important but don't know where to start. They've heard the buzzwords — machine learning, neural networks, predictive analytics — but have no practical path to applying any of it.

Kalibre bridges that gap. We sit down with your team, understand your data and your goals, and give you a concrete plan. No jargon. No upselling. Just honest ML expertise applied to your actual problem.

Founded in Port Harcourt, we work with clients across Nigeria and beyond — from early-stage startups to established businesses ready to make their data work harder.
